# MavNext — Demo Guide (about 3 minutes)

Everything here uses fictional data in the **demo workspace**, stored only in the browser under its own key. It never touches a real workspace or an earlier draft.

1. **Open the app → Explore demo.** You are viewing as *Maya Lin, President (Administrator)* of Riverside Robotics Society. The yellow bar labels the demo and the simulated "View as" switch.
2. **Overview.** Note the two upcoming meetings, Maya's open tasks, the President handoff at 8 of 24 questions, and recent activity. All of these come from the same records the other pages use.
3. **Meetings & Events → New meeting or event.** Create one dated in the next few days, then add an agenda item on its page. Return to Overview: the upcoming count went up by one.
4. **Action items tab → Add action item.** Assign it to Maya. It appears on **Tasks** as the same record. Tick it done; the Overview count drops.
5. **Handoffs → President → Edit.** Walk through the sections on the left. Type in an answer (it autosaves), and try **Unknown / Not applicable / Needs follow-up**. Open **Related records** to see which task and meeting notes are included.
6. **Review & submit.** "Reimbursements" and "First steps" are required and empty, so submit is disabled. Fill them, return, confirm David Kim as the recipient, and submit. You land on the dated **Version 1** snapshot.
7. **View as → David Kim (Incoming).** Overview says a handoff is ready for review. Open it and choose **Request clarification** or **Acknowledge receipt**. The acknowledgment dialog says it does not certify accuracy or account transfer, and David's permission stays "Incoming".
8. **Archive.** The earlier Treasurer handoff (Priya Shah → Jordan Smith) shows how acknowledged versions are kept by term and role.
9. **Organization selector** (as Maya): switch to *Women in Computing* to see a brand-new workspace with empty states and no invented activity.
10. **Settings → Reset demo workspace** restores the sample data. The dialog lists what is not affected.

Talking points:
- Accounts, invitations, and shared delivery are **simulated** in the demo. Real accounts require the Supabase setup in `backend-setup.md`, which is not yet configured or verified.
- MavNext is an independent student pilot, not a UTA service.
